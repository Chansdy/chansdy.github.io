abstract: >-
  3D object detection (3DOD) requires effective knowledge transfer while maintaining computational efficiency. We propose H2RKD, a joint homophily and heterophily relational knowledge distillation framework that integrates global and local relational knowledge to enhance intra-object similarity and inter-object discrimination. Extensive experiments on KITTI and nuScenes demonstrate the effectiveness of H2RKD for efficient 3D object detection.
