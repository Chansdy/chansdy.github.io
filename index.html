Research Projects

Monocular 3D Visual Grounding

Multimodal Spatial Understanding

Developed multi-level visual-text semantic alignment for monocular 3D grounding, combining scene, attribute and spatial semantics with visual and depth representations. On Mono3DRefer, the approach improved far-distance and hard-occlusion performance by 6.9 and 4.8 percentage points.

Zero-Shot 3D Visual Grounding

VLM-based Cross-view Reasoning

Developed a pose-guided cross-view reasoning framework using multimodal large models for query understanding and camera-pose-aware view selection. On ScanRefer and Nr3D, the zero-shot system reduced model size by at least 9× while improving localization accuracy by up to 7.9 and 10.6 percentage points.

Relational Knowledge Distillation for 3D Detection

3D Perception · Model Compression

Designed joint homophily and heterophily relational knowledge distillation for sparse point-cloud 3D detection, modeling global and local object relations. Achieved about 4× parameter compression on KITTI and nuScenes while improving mAP.

Video Virtual Try-On

Diffusion · Video Generation

Studied clothing-human spatial misalignment and text-guided diffusion generation for video virtual try-on. Led an internship team to develop a minority-costume virtual try-on system and achieved SSIM 0.95 and FID 1.59 on VVT experiments.
