About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Key Laboratory of Transportation Big Data and Artificial Intelligence, Ministry of Education, School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Jiaotong University, advised by Prof. Congyan Lang .

My research interests focus on 3D Vision and Vision-Language Models, particularly 3D Object Detection and 3D Visual Grounding. I aim to develop multimodal approaches for understanding and reasoning about complex 3D environments.

I was previously a Visiting Ph.D. Student at Nanyang Technological University, where I conducted research related to multimodal vision and 3D scene understanding.
